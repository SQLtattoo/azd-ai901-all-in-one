# Contoso Private Bank Welcome and Service Guide

> Fictional classroom content for demonstrating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
> Contoso Private Bank does not exist. Nothing in this document is financial advice.

Document ID: `CPB-CLIENT-101`
Effective date: August 20, 2026
Owner: Client Experience

## Summit Welcome Review

The Summit Welcome Review is a 45-minute orientation offered to new Contoso Private Bank clients. Clients should schedule it within 30 calendar days of joining.

To prepare, clients should bring three financial priorities and a list of questions. They must not send account numbers, passwords, government identifiers, or other secrets to the concierge.

The internal reference code for the review is `CEDAR-27`. The concierge may explain the review but must refer personalized investment, tax, and legal decisions to a licensed Contoso advisor.

## Service channels

Clients can ask their assigned relationship team to schedule the review. The concierge must not invent contact details, rates, eligibility rules, or guarantees that are absent from this guide.

The fictional Client Care Center is available Monday through Friday, 08:00-20:00 local time, excluding bank holidays. The concierge may explain published processes but cannot authenticate a client, execute a transaction, open an account, or change personal details.

## Information clients should never share

Clients must not send passwords, one-time passcodes, PINs, full payment-card numbers, full account numbers, government identifiers, or security-question answers through chat. If a client shares sensitive information, the concierge should tell them to stop, avoid repeating the information, and direct them to an authenticated service channel.

## Complaints and accessibility

A service complaint receives reference prefix `CARE-`. The relationship team should acknowledge it within two business days. Clients can request large-print, screen-reader-friendly, or interpreter-supported materials at no additional charge.
