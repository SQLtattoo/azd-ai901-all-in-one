# Contoso Private Bank Deposit Products

> Fictional classroom content for demonstrating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
> Contoso Private Bank does not exist. Rates, limits, and products are invented.

Document ID: `CPB-PRODUCT-220`
Effective date: September 1, 2026
Owner: Deposit Products

## Horizon Reserve Account

The Horizon Reserve Account is a fictional interest-bearing savings account for clients who maintain a minimum opening deposit of $10,000.

- Annual percentage yield (APY): 3.15% variable.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $18.
- Fee waiver: the fee is waived when the daily balance remains at or above $15,000 for the entire statement cycle.
- Included withdrawals: six assisted withdrawals per statement cycle.
- Excess assisted-withdrawal fee: $7 per withdrawal after the sixth.
- Product code: `HR-315`.

Rates are variable and may change after account opening. The concierge must state the effective date when quoting a rate and must not promise future returns.

## Orchard Everyday Account

The Orchard Everyday Account is a fictional transaction account intended for routine payments.

- Minimum opening deposit: $250.
- Monthly maintenance fee: $12.
- Fee waiver: either one qualifying direct deposit of at least $1,500 per month or an average monthly balance of at least $2,500.
- Domestic ATM fee charged by Contoso: $0. Third-party ATM operators may charge their own fees.
- Product code: `OE-250`.

## Northstar 12-Month Certificate

The Northstar Certificate is a fictional 12-month time deposit.

- Minimum opening deposit: $5,000.
- APY: 4.05% fixed for the 12-month term.
- Grace period: seven calendar days after maturity.
- Early withdrawal penalty: 90 days of simple interest on the amount withdrawn.
- Product code: `NS-12-405`.

The penalty may reduce principal if accrued interest is insufficient. Exceptions require review by a licensed banker and are not guaranteed.

## Product comparison boundary

The concierge may compare published features and perform simple arithmetic using these figures. It must not recommend which product is best for a particular client or infer suitability from age, income, family status, or account balances. Personalized recommendations require a licensed Contoso advisor.
