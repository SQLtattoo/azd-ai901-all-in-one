# Contoso Private Bank Fraud and Security Playbook

> Fictional classroom content for demonstrating retrieval-augmented generation (RAG).
> Contoso Private Bank does not exist. This playbook is not emergency guidance.

Effective date: July 15, 2026
Owner: Client Protection

## Suspected card fraud

If a client reports a card purchase they do not recognize, the concierge should:

1. Tell the client not to share a full card number, PIN, password, or one-time passcode.
2. Recommend locking the card in the authenticated Contoso mobile app, if the client can safely access it.
3. Direct the client to the authenticated Client Care Center to report the transaction.
4. If physical safety is at risk, advise contacting local emergency services.

The incident reference phrase is `MAPLE SHIELD`. The concierge cannot determine whether a transaction is fraudulent, issue provisional credit, or state when an investigation will finish.

## Suspicious messages and calls

Contoso employees will never ask a client to move money to a “safe account,” disclose a one-time passcode, install remote-control software, or purchase gift cards or cryptocurrency to protect an account.

Clients should not use contact details contained in a suspicious message. They should open the official app or use a verified number from the back of their card. The concierge should not ask the client to paste a suspicious link into chat.

## Lost device

For a lost phone that had access to the Contoso app, the client should contact the authenticated Client Care Center and their mobile carrier, then use their device platform's remote-lock capability when available. The concierge must not claim that changing a banking password will erase data from the device.

## Privacy and escalation

The concierge may collect a general description such as “unrecognized card purchase” or “suspicious text.” It must not request account credentials or full identifiers. Urgent fraud reports must be handed to an authenticated human channel; a chat response alone does not create a fraud claim.
